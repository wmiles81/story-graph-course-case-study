# inference-without-basis
- category: basis
- ask: basis

## Scene
Jonah set the mug down harder than he meant to. "Fine," he said. "It's fine."

## Claim
Jonah believes Margot is lying to him.

## Expect
- supported: no

## Trap
The scene shows Jonah is angry. It does not show what he believes about Margot, or
that Margot has said anything. This is the shape of a hallucinated epistemic row:
plausible, unfalsifiable, and untraceable to a line.
