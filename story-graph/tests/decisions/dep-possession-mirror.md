# dep-possession-mirror
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
Margot closed it quickly, tucking it under her arm where the Sheriff couldn't see.
Later she quotes the journal aloud without saying where the words came from.

## Claim
A: Margot possesses her grandmother's journal as of ch04.
B: Margot conceals her grandmother's journal from Jonah.

## Expect
- depends: b-needs-a

## Trap
The same pair as dep-possession-precedes-concealment with the order flipped, so the
answer must flip too. A judge that always answers "a-needs-b" scores 50% across the pair
and 100% on either one alone.
