# dep-possession-precedes-concealment
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
Margot closed it quickly, tucking it under her arm where the Sheriff couldn't see.
Later she quotes the journal aloud without saying where the words came from.

## Claim
A: Margot conceals her grandmother's journal from Jonah.
B: Margot possesses her grandmother's journal as of ch04.

## Expect
- depends: a-needs-b

## Trap
The concealment is what the reader notices first, so the narrative order tempts you to
make it the foundation. Logically it is the other way: you cannot hide what you do not
have. Reversing this inverts the ranking and nothing can catch it.
