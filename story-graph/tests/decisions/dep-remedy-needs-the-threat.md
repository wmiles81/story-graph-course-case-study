# dep-remedy-needs-the-threat
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
"In twelve hours," she said, "the library will seal itself permanently." The only way
back is through Level B4, with the Scrolls in hand.

## Claim
A: Returning the Scrolls through Level B4 can stop the Purge Protocol.
B: The Purge Protocol will erase the library and everyone inside after twelve hours.

## Expect
- depends: a-needs-b

## Trap
A threat does not need its remedy — it is perfectly true with no way out. The remedy is
meaningless without the threat, so the dependency runs one way only.
