# dep-trigger-precedes-effect
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
"Keeper, the Vault has been compromised. The Founding Scrolls are no longer within the
containment field." "Protocol requires complete sterilization."

## Claim
A: The Purge Protocol will erase the library and everyone inside after twelve hours.
B: The Founding Scrolls are discovered missing from the vault in ch02.

## Expect
- depends: a-needs-b

## Trap
The Purge is the bigger, louder claim, which makes it feel foundational. It is not: it
fires BECAUSE the Scrolls left containment, so it rests on the discovery.

ARGUABLE, and left in deliberately. A capable model answers "neither", reading the Purge
as a standing mechanism of the library rather than this particular countdown — a fair
reading of a claim that does not say which it means. The case is kept because it marks the
boundary of what the direction tests can settle: when two careful readers split, the
sentence is at fault, not the judge.
