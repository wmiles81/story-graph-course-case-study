# dep-trigger-precedes-effect
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
"The Purge Protocol," she repeated, her voice numb. "It's a failsafe. If someone triggers
the containment wards without authorization... if someone tries to steal from the Vault..."
"Keeper, the Vault has been compromised. The Founding Scrolls are no longer within the
containment field." "Protocol requires complete sterilization."

## Claim
A: The Purge Protocol activates on the ch02 vault breach and will erase the library within twelve hours.
B: The Founding Scrolls are discovered missing from the vault in ch02.

## Expect
- depends: a-needs-b

## Trap
The Purge is the bigger, louder claim, which makes it feel foundational. It is not: this
activation happens BECAUSE the Scrolls left containment, so it rests on the discovery.

The case earned its keep by being WRONG first. Phrased as "The Purge Protocol will erase
the library after twelve hours", two careful readers split — the prose calls the Protocol
"a failsafe", so that sentence can mean the standing mechanism, which depends on nothing,
or this particular countdown, which depends on the breach. Naming the activation settles
it. When judges disagree about direction, suspect the sentence before the judge.
