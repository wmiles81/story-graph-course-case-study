# dep-neither-same-scene
- category: dependency
- ask: dependency

## Scene
Every door in the building sealed itself shut. The wolf inside Jonah's chest rumbled,
and he knew her scent the way he knew the scent of pine and snow and home.

## Claim
A: The library seals itself.
B: Jonah's wolf recognises Margot as pack.
## Expect
- depends: neither

## Trap
Both happen in the same scene and one is causally dramatic, which invites a link. Neither
claim needs the other to be true; co-occurrence is not dependency.
