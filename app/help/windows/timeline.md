# Timeline

Chronology by chapter: story-time and elapsed time, drawn as a track.

The timeline is only as good as the Timeline section of the graph. A gap here usually means
the chapter was never committed, not that nothing happened in it — check the **Canon Commit
Log** to tell those apart.

Timeline answers the questions that make continuity fail quietly:

- How much time has passed since the injury?
- Is this character in two places within a span that allows no travel?
- Does an emotional reaction land three chapters after the event that caused it?

Those are logistics questions, and `audit` (in **Reports**) runs detectors for several of
them mechanically.
